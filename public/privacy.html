Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy covers vasumehra.in and the private sites hosted on its subdomains, run by Vasu Mehra as personal projects.

Signing in

Some parts of this site are private and ask you to sign in with Google. Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access; Google confirms your identity and shares your email address and basic profile details. Your password is never seen by this site.

Your email address is used for one purpose only: to check it against a short list of people allowed to open that private site. It is not used for marketing, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

What gets stored

  • A sign-in cookie in your browser, so you are not asked to sign in on every page.
  • Access logs held by Cloudflare, which record the email address that signed in and when. These are used to see who has access and to spot misuse.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts.

Service providers

The sites are hosted on Cloudflare, and sign-in uses Google. Each handles your data under its own privacy policy: Cloudflare and Google.

Your choices

You can ask for your access to be removed, or for any stored record of your sign-ins to be deleted, at any time. Revoking this site's access to your Google account is done from your Google account permissions page.
